## Main improvements

1. **Make IAI the center of the resume.** The revised versions name the AI document application, UEI signal processing, GVDM, robotics integration, image-based landing software, UAV control modernization, and Avim monitoring. These examples are stronger than broad claims about high-quality code or advanced AI tools.
2. **Use a concrete summary.** Lead with C/C++, real-time and embedded software, aerospace and robotics, and responsibility across requirements, architecture, testing, integration, and documentation. The revised summary also identifies your current AI application.
3. **Show responsibility and outcomes.** Describe the work you performed and the resulting capability. Examples already supported by your documents include standardized critical modules, improved maintainability, software in use by the Air Force, and documented verification findings before human approval.
4. **Add measured results where you can substantiate them.** Useful figures might include performance improvements, reduced defects or maintenance effort, number of modules modernized, or translation-review time. The current sources provide no such figures, so the drafts do not invent them. The AI document's reference to hundreds of documents is a description of intended product scale, not proof of completed translation volume.
5. **Keep the engineering skills easy to scan.** Languages, operating systems, communication protocols, Qt, build tools, source control, and analysis tools carry more weight than a long list of editors and general productivity products. The shorter resume trims the list; the comprehensive version retains the broader inventory.
6. **Condense earlier experience for applications.** Teaching, research, Intel, police, maritime, and military experience remain in the documents. The shorter version gives more space to recent technical work and omits older training and secondary-school qualifications.
7. **Use consistent English and formatting.** The revised resumes add a prominent name and role, clickable contact links, international phone formatting, consistent dates, and clearer technical labels such as embedded Linux, Boost.Asio, and RS-232. The standalone translation retains the greeting and signature; the integrated resumes use normal resume sections.
